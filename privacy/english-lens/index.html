English Lens privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026. This page describes how English Lens handles information as of the date above. It is not legal advice.

Scope

This policy applies to the English Lens iPhone app and the EchoFlow services that process text you submit through the app or the Learn with EchoFlow Action extension.

Account and authentication

Supabase provides email-based authentication for English Lens. Supabase stores your account email and authentication tokens needed to sign you in. EchoFlow Labs does not operate a separate password database for this flow.

Text you submit

When you share, paste, or type text in English Lens, that selected user content is sent to the EchoFlow backend and to a large language model (LLM) to generate translation, explanations, and retelling results. Processing is tied to your request—not to build a browsable history inside the app.

English Lens does not read text from other apps unless you explicitly share or enter it.

On your device

  • The iOS client does not store a local history of past prompts or results.
  • Source text is not placed in ordinary logs, analytics payloads, or crash metadata.

On our servers

  • The backend may cache a normalized, validated result in Redis for up to seven days, subject to eviction for cost or latency. This cache is operational—not a user-visible history feature.
  • A usage ledger in the database stores aggregate identifiers such as token counts, cost figures, and request identifiers. It does not store full prompt or result text in that ledger.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use English Lens for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Retention and deletion

Cached results in Redis expire within the window described above or sooner when evicted. Authentication data is retained as long as your account exists unless you ask us to delete it.

To request account deletion or ask questions about data we hold, email harvey@eflabs.tech. We will verify requests using reasonable means (for example, your registered email).

Changes

We may update this page when practices change. Material updates will be reflected here with a revised date.
