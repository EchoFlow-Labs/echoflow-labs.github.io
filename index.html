English Lens

Built for the text you already have open—selected, shared, pasted, or typed—not for reading behind other apps.

English Lens is an iPhone app targeting iOS 26. App Store launch is in progress; we will announce availability here when the listing is live.

  • Learn with EchoFlow Action extension in the share sheet actions list: translation, explanations, and retelling for selected plain text you share from a supporting host app.
  • Paste or type in the main app when sharing is not convenient.
  • Plain-text input only—formatted or rich content may be normalized before processing.

Privacy note: English Lens does not inject into other apps’ text menus or read content you have not shared. See our English Lens privacy page for how data is handled.
